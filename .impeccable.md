# Design context

- Audience: HR teams and founders evaluating Falak for frontend and product-engineering roles; developers are a secondary audience.
- Primary outcome: make Falak's frontend judgement, production experience, and ability to ship polished products immediately credible, then make contact effortless.
- Voice: precise, calm, technically confident, and human. Avoid cartoonish copy, decorative UI, inflated claims, and generic portfolio language.
- Visual direction: a narrow editorial canvas, Geist typography, neutral surfaces, `#5CBEFF` used as a restrained accent, compact controls, flat lists, and generous section rhythm.
- Interaction direction: immediate navigation; 50-180ms feedback; optional list/showcase views; previews only on intentional hover/focus; reduced-motion support; no blocking page transitions.
- Content direction: lead with shipped work and outcomes, keep a refined personal backstory, show the working stack on About, and use progressive disclosure for project detail.
