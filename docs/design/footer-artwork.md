# Mumbai waterfront footer

An original illustration generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for the site. The scene is decorative; contact links, local time, visitor count, and navigation remain ordinary accessible HTML. The optional paper boat uses CSS motion only after activation. Reduced-motion users receive the same completion feedback without spatial movement.

Asset: `public/images/mumbai-waterfront.webp` (2172 × 724).

Inspiration: [Footer Design's illustrative collection](https://www.footer.design/styles/illustrative), especially [Leedlime](https://www.footer.design/sites/leedlime), and the scenery framing [Pawan's portfolio](https://pawann.dev/). No assets were copied from those sites.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original exceptionally refined panoramic illustration for the bottom of a personal software engineer portfolio from Mumbai. Asset only, absolutely no text, no typography, no UI, no border or watermark. Very wide 3:1 panoramic composition (ideally 2304x768). A quiet Mumbai waterfront imagined as an exquisitely detailed monochrome blue risograph / fine ordered-dither etching on pure white paper. Recognizable Bandra-Worli Sea Link cable towers toward the left, delicate layered distant Mumbai skyscraper skyline across the middle and right, small architectural shapes. Vast tranquil open Arabian Sea in foreground rendered with sparse short blue horizontal etched ripples and fine pixel stipples. Low horizon: buildings occupy a band from about 40% to 65% of image height; the upper 35% is entirely blank pure white, with the skyline gently emerging from the white. A faint hazy small sun in upper right near horizon. Frame edges and foreground dissolve into white fine stippling, no hard bounding edges. A few darker blue foreground rocks at extreme bottom left and bottom right add depth but keep central sea uncluttered for a small animated paper boat to be added with code. Strict limited tonal palette white and muted slate / steel blue ink (#53798e, #96b1bf, pale #dce8ef); crisp beautiful engraving detail, visible dither grain at close view, elegant architectural precision and atmospheric haze at distance. No purple, no pink, no neon, no glow, no saturated color, no large foreground objects, no existing boats, no people, no cartoon. Understated, contemplative, crafted, high-end editorial, intended as an airy seamless footer landscape, not a rectangular hero picture.
