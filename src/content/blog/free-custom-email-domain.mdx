Paying $6+ every month for a workspace mailbox just to put `hi@yourdomain.com` on a resume or cold email makes zero sense when you already own a domain.

Yet emailing recruiters or founders from `firstname.lastname99@gmail.com` when your portfolio sits on `yourdomain.dev` feels needlessly amateur.

This is the exact $0 setup I run for **hi@falakgala.dev**: incoming mail forwards straight into Gmail via Cloudflare Email Routing, and outgoing replies send from **hi@falakgala.dev** right inside the same Gmail inbox.

## What You'll Have in 15 Minutes

- **hi@yourdomain.com** receiving mail and forwarding directly into your personal Gmail
- Gmail sending new emails and replies **from** **hi@yourdomain.com**
- Your portfolio hosting (Vercel, Cloudflare Workers, or anywhere else) left completely untouched

## Prerequisites

- A domain you own (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, Porkbun, etc.)
- A Gmail account you use as your main inbox
- A free [Cloudflare](https://cloudflare.com) account

## How the Pieces Fit Together

Three separate layers handle your domain:

1. **Domain Registrar** — where you *bought* the domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
2. **Nameservers** — who *manages* your DNS records (we'll move DNS to Cloudflare)
3. **Hosting** — where your website *lives* (stays untouched)

Your domain stays registered wherever you bought it. Only DNS moves to Cloudflare, which gives you free Email Routing without breaking your site.

## Step 1: Add Your Domain to Cloudflare

1. Sign up or log in at [cloudflare.com](https://cloudflare.com).
2. Click **Add a Site** → enter your domain → select the **Free plan**.
3. Let Cloudflare scan your existing DNS records.

**Important:** Cloudflare sometimes imports stale `A` records from your registrar instead of your actual hosting target. Remove any leftover registrar parking `A` records, and make sure your site records point to your real host (for example, on Vercel):

```text
Type    Name    Content                 Proxy Status
CNAME   @       cname.vercel-dns.com    DNS only (grey cloud)
CNAME   www     cname.vercel-dns.com    DNS only (grey cloud)
```

*(If your site is hosted on Vercel, keep the proxy status as **DNS only (grey cloud)** so Vercel's TLS certificate renewal doesn't conflict. Keep any existing `CAA` or `TXT` verification records intact.)*

Click **Continue to activation** and copy the **2 nameservers** Cloudflare gives you.

## Step 2: Update Nameservers on Your Registrar

1. Log into your registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.) → open your domain's **DNS settings**.
2. Find the **Nameservers** section → choose **Custom / I'll use my own nameservers**.
3. Replace the existing nameservers with the two Cloudflare nameservers.
4. Save changes.

Propagation usually takes 5–30 minutes. Your site stays live during the switch, and Cloudflare emails you once the domain is active.

## Step 3: Set Up Cloudflare Email Routing

Once Cloudflare marks your domain **Active**:

1. Open your domain in Cloudflare → go to **Email → Email Routing**.
2. Click **Get Started**.
3. Review the `MX` and `TXT` (SPF) records Cloudflare needs, then click **Add records and Enable**.

Now create your custom forwarding address:

1. Open the **Routing Rules** tab → click **+ Create Address**.
2. Custom address: **`hi`** (which creates **hi@yourdomain.com**).
3. Action: **Send to an email** → enter your personal Gmail address → **Save**.

Click the verification link Cloudflare sends to your Gmail. Any email sent to **hi@yourdomain.com** now lands straight in your Gmail inbox.

## Step 4: Send From Your Custom Email Inside Gmail

Receiving works—now configure Gmail to send *from* **hi@yourdomain.com** too.

### Generate a Gmail App Password

Google blocks plain account passwords for SMTP, so create an app-specific one:

1. Ensure **2-Step Verification** is enabled on your Google account.
2. Open [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords).
3. Name it something clear like `Custom Email` → click **Create**.
4. Copy the **16-character password** immediately (Google shows it once).

### Add Your Custom Address in Gmail

1. Open Gmail → click the **gear icon** → **See all settings**.
2. Open the **Accounts and Import** tab.
3. Under **"Send mail as"** → click **Add another email address**.
4. Enter **Your Name** and **hi@yourdomain.com**, **uncheck "Treat as an alias"**, and click **Next Step**.
5. Fill in the SMTP settings:
   - **SMTP Server:** `smtp.gmail.com`
   - **Port:** `587`
   - **Username:** `yourname@gmail.com` (your actual Gmail address)
   - **Password:** the 16-character App Password
   - **Secured connection:** TLS
6. Click **Add Account**.
7. Gmail sends a verification email to **hi@yourdomain.com**, which forwards right into your inbox—enter the code or click the link to confirm.

### Make It Your Default Sender

Back under **Settings → Accounts and Import → Send mail as**, click **make default** beside **hi@yourdomain.com** (or select *"Reply from the same address the message was sent to"*).
