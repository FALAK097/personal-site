macOS gets the big architecture pieces right and still leaves annoying defaults everywhere else. Plug in a non-Apple mouse and natural scrolling fights your trackpad. Join a video call and find out after clicking *Join* that your lighting looks washed out. Try sending a 200 MB build artifact to a Linux box or Android phone and AirDrop hits a dead end.

In [Part 1, I listed the 8 developer and AI workflow apps I rely on every day](/writing/mac-apps-for-developers-and-ai-workflows). This half covers the everyday utilities that smooth out input devices, acoustics, calls, external displays, and local file sharing—without bloated background suites.

## 1. LinearMouse — Fix External Mouse Behavior Without 500 MB Suites

macOS couples external scroll direction to trackpad settings and applies aggressive pointer acceleration that makes fine cursor movement feel floaty. Most hardware vendors ask you to install half a gigabyte of background updater software to override it.

[LinearMouse](https://linearmouse.app/) is free, open-source, and handles per-device, per-app, and per-display rules cleanly. You can disable pointer acceleration and reverse scroll wheel direction on an external mouse while leaving your built-in trackpad untouched.

## 2. Thock — Crisp Keyboard Feedback That Mutes for Music

Typing eight hours a day directly on a flat MacBook keyboard can feel flat when you are away from a mechanical board. [Thock](https://thockapp.com/) adds subtle, low-latency mechanical keystroke acoustics and automatically pauses playback whenever Spotify or Apple Music is active so sound profiles never clash with audio.

```bash
brew install --cask kamillobinski/thock/thock
```

## 3. Hand Mirror — One-Click Camera Check Before Calls

Opening Photo Booth or joining a Meet link early just to verify framing and lighting takes too many steps. [Hand Mirror](https://handmirror.app/) puts one small icon in your menu bar: click once for an instant camera preview, click again to close it before jumping onto a call.

## 4. Cap — Open-Source Screen Recording Without Monthly Per-Seat Fees

Recording bug reproductions or quick product walkthroughs shouldn't force another monthly screen-recorder subscription. [Cap](https://cap.so/) is open-source and handles both **Instant Mode** (quick shareable links) and **Studio Mode** (local 4K recordings with auto-zoom, backgrounds, and captions).

## 5. Granola — Clean Call Notes Without an Awkward Meeting Bot

Having *"So-and-so's AI Notetaker has joined the meeting"* pop into customer calls changes the dynamic immediately. [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) captures system audio directly on your Mac without dialing a bot into the room, letting you jot down rough bullets during the call and merging them with the transcript afterward.

## 6. LocalSend — Cross-Platform AirDrop Over Local Wi-Fi

AirDrop stops helping the moment you need to hand a screen recording, APK, or log bundle to an Android phone, Linux workstation, or Windows laptop. [LocalSend](https://localsend.org/) is free, open-source, works over your local Wi-Fi network with zero cloud uploads or accounts, and even exposes a temporary local browser download link if the receiving device doesn't have the app installed.

## 7. BetterDisplay + AltTab — Sharp External Monitors and Real Window Switching

If you work docked to an external monitor, two focused utilities fix macOS's sharpest desktop rough edges:

- **BetterDisplay** unlocks crisp HiDPI scaling modes and native hardware brightness (DDC) controls on external displays that macOS would otherwise treat awkwardly.
- **AltTab** brings per-window thumbnail switching to `Alt+Tab` (or `Cmd+Tab`), so switching between two separate Ghostty, VS Code, or browser windows stops requiring extra `Cmd+~` cycles.

```bash
brew install --cask betterdisplay alt-tab
```

## 8. Wallspace — Native Animated Wallpapers Without Wasting Battery

Most animated wallpaper wrappers lean on webviews or heavy background loops that wake your GPU constantly. **Wallspace** uses native Swift and AVFoundation across multiple monitors so subtle ambient loops stay gentle on fans and unplugged battery life.
